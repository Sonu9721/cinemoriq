# Strategic foundation

## Recommended category

**AI Commercial Systems Studio**

## Core positioning

> We help industrial companies launch complex products through cinematic AI content and convert that attention into qualified enquiries using custom software and automation.

Short version:

> We create demand with cinematic AI—and capture it with custom software.

Homepage hero:

> Make complex products impossible to ignore—and easier to buy.

## Business model

The agency has two connected capabilities:

1. **Cinematic Demand**
   - AI films
   - Cinematic compositing
   - Product visualization
   - Launch campaigns
   - Localization and content variants

2. **Intelligent Operations**
   - Lead qualification
   - Product advisors
   - Configurators and ROI calculators
   - CRM routing
   - Quote/RFQ workflows
   - Internal automation and reporting

They are connected through the **Demand-to-Deal Engine**:

> Cinematic creative → landing page/QR/configurator → AI qualification → CRM routing and human follow-up → qualified opportunity/revenue → performance data → next creative test.

## Non-negotiable positioning rules

- Do not sell a generic list of AI tools.
- Do not lead with Kling, Veo, Seedance or other model names.
- Do not describe the business as two unrelated services.
- Sell a measurable commercial outcome.
- Use one visible pain to enter an account; cross-sell the second capability later.
- Never invent results, logos, testimonials, views or conversion claims.
- Never promise guaranteed virality or revenue.

# Phase 7 — Discovery, proposals and closing

**Target:** Days 31–65

## Step 22 — Quantify the business problem

On every discovery call establish:

- Why now?
- Current process and baseline
- Monthly volume
- Delay, error and rework
- Commercial impact
- Business owner and budget owner
- Systems, APIs and data constraints
- Creative asset/likeness/music rights
- Security and compliance requirements
- Pilot acceptance criteria
- Other decision-makers
- Procurement/legal timeline

- [ ] Do not send a full implementation proposal if the problem cannot be qualified.

Suggested opening:

> I’d like to validate the current process, quantify the impact and decide whether a small paid pilot is justified. If it isn’t, I’ll say so.

## Step 23 — Sell the correct next step

- [ ] Recommend a paid diagnostic when material unknowns remain.
- [ ] Recommend a pilot before a large rollout.
- [ ] Book the proposal-review meeting during the call.
- [ ] Avoid orphaned proposals with no next meeting.

## Step 24 — Use a three-option proposal

- Option A: Commercial Opportunity Sprint
- Option B: One Product–One Market Pilot
- Option C: Full Demand-to-Deal Rollout

Every proposal must include:

- Desired outcome
- Verified baseline
- Scope and out-of-scope
- Deliverables
- Timeline
- Acceptance criteria
- Client responsibilities
- Security and IP
- ROI assumptions
- Payment terms
- Decision date

## Step 25 — Handle objections without weakening positioning

If the price is challenged:

> Is the concern total cash exposure, or that the expected value has not been proven yet? We can reduce Phase 1 scope, but we will not randomly reduce the same scope and quality.

If a free sample is requested:

> The low-risk option is a small paid concept sprint. Full custom spec work is the actual service.

If an ROI guarantee is requested:

> We can guarantee agreed deliverables, approval gates and correction of acceptance issues. We cannot honestly guarantee revenue or virality because distribution, pricing and sales execution also affect the outcome.

# Hard-truth rules

- Model access is not a moat.
- A dual skill set is valuable, but it becomes defensible only through vertical expertise, proof, reusable systems and distribution.
- Foreign clients are not automatically high-paying clients.
- A beautiful film without distribution and measurement is not a revenue strategy.
- A software demo without reliability, monitoring, security and support is not an enterprise solution.
- Generic cold email is weak; trigger-based, lawful and relevant outreach can work.
- Free full custom films for every prospect are unsustainable and risky.
- International buyers care about communication, contracts, security, IP, continuity and execution certainty.
- White-label partners can create fast revenue but may reduce margin and public case-study rights.
- Industrial deals can take time; the agency partner channel should help balance cash flow.
- No proof means six-figure deals are possible but not probable.
- Do not hire ahead of repeatable demand.
- Do not scale a process that has not produced measurable wins.
