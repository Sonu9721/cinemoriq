## One factual observation

Write one specific, externally visible fact. Example: the company is launching a named machine at a confirmed booth, or its event page routes every visitor to a generic form.

> Observation:

Do not claim its CRM is slow, leads are being lost or conversion is poor unless the buyer confirms it.

## One commercial hypothesis

Connect the observation to a possible outcome without pretending it is proven.

> Hypothesis: A [specific cinematic asset] connected to [specific qualification/routing workflow] could help [buyer role] increase/shorten/measure [business metric].

## One intelligent question

Ask a question that could disprove the hypothesis.

> Question:

Good examples:

- How are booth enquiries currently separated into information requests, demo-ready prospects and quote-ready opportunities?
- Is the primary launch target dealer engagement, booked demonstrations or completed RFQs?
- Which product/application details must sales receive before a first useful conversation?

## Personal opener

> Hi [First Name]—I noticed [verified trigger/observation]. I have one idea for connecting [specific launch story] to [qualified meeting/RFQ outcome], but I do not want to assume your current process is broken. May I ask how [intelligent question]?

## Proof and risk check

- Can we show a relevant proof asset honestly?
- Have we avoided confidential or unapproved product claims?
- Is every visual concept physically/product accurate?
- Are we requesting a conversation rather than offering unpaid custom production?
- Is the next step small and clear?
