## 1. The commercial goal

The next 90 days are not about looking like a large global agency. They are about creating real commercial proof:

- 1–3 legitimate paying international clients.
- One measurable international case study.
- One project converted into a recurring retainer.
- One dependable foreign agency partner.

The scoreboard is revenue, qualified conversations, proof and repeat business—not followers, tool knowledge or website polish.

## 2. What we do, in simple language

We help industrial businesses do two connected things:

1. Present a complex product through premium cinematic content so buyers notice it, understand it and trust the company.
2. Build software and automation that captures interested buyers, asks the right questions, routes them to sales and improves follow-up.

Simple example:

> A machinery company launches a new machine at a trade show. We create the cinematic launch film that attracts attention. We then create the QR/landing-page, qualification and CRM workflow that converts booth interest into complete RFQs and sales meetings.

We are not selling “an AI video plus some software.” We are selling a shorter, more measurable journey from product attention to a qualified sales opportunity.

## 3. Niche decision — locked for 90 days

### Primary direct niche — 70% of effort

US and UK companies in:

- Industrial equipment.
- Machine tools and manufacturing technology.
- Robotics and industrial automation.
- Advanced manufacturing equipment.

### Secondary acquisition channel — 30% of effort

US, UK and UAE marketing, creative, production and web agencies that:

- Already serve industrial, automotive, B2B or high-consideration brands.
- Need a reliable white-label AI production or automation partner.
- Have credible client relationships but no deep combined capability in cinematic AI and custom software.

### Opportunistic only

Dubai real estate and hospitality will be pursued only through a warm introduction, credible local partner or unusually strong inbound opportunity.

### Focus rule

Do not change the primary niche because a few prospects ignore or reject outreach. Review the niche only after meaningful evidence exists—for example, at least five buyer interviews and a properly executed outreach sample to qualified accounts. Messaging, offer and proof should be corrected before the niche is blamed.

## 5. Ideal Customer Profile

### Core account criteria

| Attribute | Strong-fit signal |
|---|---|
| Geography | United States or United Kingdom |
| Company size | Approximately 50–1,000 employees; use this as guidance, not a blind rule |
| Product | Expensive physical equipment, preferably $25,000+ or high lifetime value |
| Sales model | B2B sales team and/or dealer/distributor network |
| Timing | Product launch, IMTS/other trade show, expansion or campaign within roughly 120 days |
| Buying journey | PDF-heavy pages, weak product video, generic contact form or slow/manual RFQ workflow |
| Internal capability | Existing marketing and sales owners who can implement and measure the project |
| Commercial capacity | Customer value and budget can support a $10,000+ implementation |
| Systems | CRM or at least a defined lead-handling process |

Company size is an estimate in public research unless an authoritative company statement is available. It must never be presented as audited fact.

### Priority buyer roles

1. VP/Director of Marketing.
2. Product Marketing Director.
3. VP/Director of Sales.
4. Dealer Development/Channel Head.
5. Digital Transformation Director.
6. COO for mid-market businesses.

The first contact should normally own the visible problem. Do not contact six executives at the same company on the same day.

### Immediate rejection rules

- Individual creators and very small local businesses.
- $500–$2,000 total-budget buyers.
- No business metric, no decision-maker access or no buying process.
- Request for a complete free prototype or unpaid custom strategy.
- “Make it viral” as the primary objective.
- Impossible deadline, no deposit or unlimited revisions.
- Unauthorized celebrity/CEO deepfake or deceptive synthetic media request.
- Refusal to clarify data, IP, security or approval responsibilities.

## 6. Account scoring — evidence-based, not optimistic

| Component | 0 points | 1 point | 2 points |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICP fit | Clear mismatch | Partial fit or important unknown | Strong match across geography, product value, size and sales model |
| Active trigger | No current trigger found | Relevant 2026 activity, but timing/participation is uncertain | Verified launch, event participation, expansion or deadline within about 120 days |
| Visible problem | No responsible observation | One useful, observable gap | Two or more observable gaps in product storytelling or enquiry flow |
| Budget signal | Weak commercial capacity | Plausible capacity, not strongly evidenced | Expensive product plus strong market/dealer/event investment signal |
| Buyer access | No verified route | Verified relevant person or credible public contact route | Not applicable; maximum is 1 |
| Relevant proof | No suitable agency proof | Relevant industrial sample/case is ready | Not applicable; maximum is 1 |

**Total:** 10 points maximum.
**Deep-personalization threshold:** 7+ points, at least medium evidence confidence, at least one observable problem point, and a credible buyer/contact route.

A high-fit company with excellent marketing and no responsible problem observation may still score 7. It belongs in research or a buyer-interview queue—not in expensive hyper-personalized outreach until a relevant gap is found.

Important: “Relevant proof” measures our readiness, not the prospect. Until an industrial concept or case is genuinely ready, this field remains **0**. A good-looking unrelated showreel is not automatically relevant proof.

### Evidence confidence

- **High:** Official company/event source supports the key trigger and a relevant buyer/contact route is verified.
- **Medium:** Core fit and trigger are supported, but employee range, buyer or buying journey includes a responsible estimate.
- **Low:** Too many important assumptions. Research again before outreach.

## 7. The initial solution hypothesis

For qualified IMTS/product-launch accounts, the starting hypothesis is:

### Launch-to-Lead System

- 15–30 second cinematic product-launch film.
- Short event/social cutdowns.
- Product landing page or event QR experience.
- Buyer qualification questions based on approved product data.
- CRM/email routing and meeting booking.
- Basic lead and follow-up reporting.

This is a hypothesis for research and personalization—not yet a fixed proposal. Buyer interviews must reveal which part has the strongest commercial value.
